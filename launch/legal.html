01 / TERMS

Terms of Service

Effective date: Not yet effective. Draft prepared 7 November 2026.

1. Operator and acceptance

GainDeep is a pre-launch software project. The legal person or business responsible for operating the service has not yet been identified on this page. These draft terms do not create a binding public offer. Before public launch, the operator's legal name, address or required business details, support contact, effective date, and governing law must be published.

2. Eligibility and third-party requirements

You may use only the features for which you meet the minimum age, identity, location, and other eligibility requirements imposed by applicable law, app stores, internet providers, and third-party services. Some network-sharing providers require an adult operator. Do not activate or operate a provider feature if you do not meet its requirements. Do not use another person's identity or account to bypass an age or eligibility rule.

3. The service

GainDeep may provide account features, activity information, optional network-sharing controls, points, points-only memberships, a daily points wheel, and reward-request tools. Features may be limited, changed, paused, or withdrawn. A public status endpoint reports known launch gates; it is not a guarantee that a feature works on a particular device or in a particular country.

4. Network sharing

Network sharing is optional and must be clearly enabled by the user. When enabled, third-party network requests may use the user's internet connection and public IP address and may consume mobile or home data. Only use it on a connection and service plan that permit this activity. The user must be able to stop sharing. GainDeep does not promise that traffic measurements identify every byte as belonging to one particular provider process. Network sharing must remain unavailable for public commercial use until provider permission, operator eligibility, and technical safeguards are confirmed.

5. Points

Points are internal service units. They are not a bank balance, deposit, security, investment, cryptocurrency, or guaranteed income. Points cannot be sold or transferred. Earning may be paused, rejected, delayed, or adjusted to correct errors or abuse. No earning rate or monetary value is guaranteed. The app must display the currently applicable point rules before a user takes an action.

6. Memberships and daily wheel

Paid membership plans are purchased with points only, last 30 days, and do not renew automatically. Membership benefits and prices are those displayed in the app at purchase. The daily wheel is limited to one spin per 24 hours and the server selects the outcome. The currently configured prize distribution is 0 points (10%), 5 (30%), 10 (30%), 15 (20%), 25 (8%), and 50 (2%). The wheel may be disabled or its rules changed before launch.

7. Reward requests

Reward requests may be manually reviewed and are not an automatic purchase or guaranteed delivery. Catalogue display does not prove local stock, regional eligibility, or fulfilment availability. The user must confirm the beneficiary email and selected item before submitting. A request may remain pending for up to 48 hours as an estimate, not a guarantee. If a request cannot be fulfilled, points will be handled according to the displayed request status and applicable law.

8. Acceptable use

You must not falsify measurements, exploit bugs, interfere with another user, access systems without permission, route unlawful traffic, evade provider restrictions, automate abuse, or use the service in violation of law or third-party terms. You must keep account credentials private and promptly report suspected compromise.

9. Suspension and termination

Access may be restricted where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users or infrastructure, comply with law, or respond to provider requirements. Where legally and operationally possible, the operator should provide an explanation and a way to request review. Account closure and deletion procedures must be published before public launch.

10. Availability, disclaimers, and liability

The service is provided on an evolving pre-launch basis. No uninterrupted availability, particular earnings, network performance, reward stock, or delivery time is promised. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Any lawful liability limits must be completed by the operator's legal reviewer before the terms become effective.

11. Changes and disputes

Changes should be dated and presented before they take effect. Governing law, dispute forum, mandatory consumer rights, and formal notices remain to be selected by the legal operator with qualified advice. They are intentionally not guessed here.

12. Contact

A public support email and any legally required operator contact details will be added before public launch. No contact address is being invented or published in this draft.

02 / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: Not yet effective. Draft prepared 7 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller or responsible legal operator has not yet been identified. That identity and a working privacy contact must be published before public launch. This draft is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific privacy notice.

2. Information that may be processed

  • Account data: email address, authentication user identifier, account confirmation state, and account security events. Password authentication is handled through the authentication provider.
  • App and account activity: points balance, streak and activity timestamps, opt-in state, membership plan and expiry, wheel results, and relevant error or diagnostic records.
  • Reward requests: selected item, country, value and currency, beneficiary email, points reserved, request status, and fulfilment or notification metadata.
  • Network-sharing records: device/account identifier, opt-in state, service status, timestamps, measured byte counts, request identifiers, and technical logs needed for accounting and abuse prevention.
  • Connection metadata: IP address and user-agent information may be processed by hosting, authentication, network, or third-party validation services as part of normal requests.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, operate requested features, record points and reward requests, prevent fraud and abuse, troubleshoot failures, secure the service, and meet legal or accounting obligations. Network-sharing data is processed only when the feature is enabled and must be subject to the provider's own terms.

4. Service providers and recipients

The current software integrates with Supabase for authentication and database services, Vercel for application hosting and API execution, and may interact with Mysterium-related network services, Bitrefill catalogue services, Gmail notifications when configured, and CryptoRefills validation when configured. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. The exact provider configuration and contracts must be reviewed before launch. Reward beneficiary details may also be viewed by the operator or authorized fulfilment staff to process a manual request.

5. Network-sharing disclosure

If network sharing is enabled, other people's requests may pass through the user's connection and appear to destination services as coming from the user's public IP address. The provider may separately process traffic and account metadata. Users should review the applicable provider terms and their internet provider's acceptable-use rules before enabling sharing.

6. International processing

The current Supabase project is configured in Australia (Sydney). Other infrastructure or service providers may process data in other countries. The operator must confirm transfer mechanisms and provide any additional disclosures required by the laws that apply to users.

7. Retention

Account, points, reward, and security records may be retained for service operation, fraud prevention, accounting, disputes, and legal obligations. A precise retention schedule and deletion workflow have not yet been verified. They must be defined and published by the operator before public launch. Do not assume that deleting the app deletes server-side account or transaction records.

8. Security

The project uses server-side authentication checks, restricted database access, and transactional controls for selected points and reward operations. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Security testing and launch configuration are still in progress; this draft does not claim that the service has passed an independent audit.

9. Your choices and rights

Users can turn off network sharing in the app and sign out. Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. A working privacy contact and verified request-handling process must be published before public launch. Some records may need to be retained where law permits or requires.

10. Children and eligibility

Age and eligibility requirements vary by feature, country, app store, and provider. Network-sharing providers may require an adult operator. The service must not be used to bypass those restrictions. The operator must set and publish a clear age policy before public launch.

11. Cookies and website analytics

The current launch page uses a same-origin status request and does not intentionally load third-party analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request logs. If cookies, analytics, or advertising are added later, this notice must be updated before they are used.

12. Updates and contact

This policy may change as the service and its providers change. A named legal operator, support email, privacy contact, retention schedule, and effective date are still required before this policy can become final.

Pre-launch gate: Have the eligible adult legal operator and a qualified privacy/legal reviewer confirm every provider, retention period, jurisdiction, and user-rights process before public release.
