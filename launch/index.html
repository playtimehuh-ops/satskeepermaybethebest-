01 / THE IDEA

Optional by design.

Sharing should only happen after a clear opt-in. A user must be able to pause it, understand that traffic may use their connection and IP address, and check what the app actually measures.

02 / THE STANDARD

Proof before promises.

Points, memberships, and reward requests must be tested end to end. A working screen or successful build is not proof of real traffic, earnings, available gift cards, or successful delivery.

Not accepting public sign-ups yet.

Network-sharing and buyer traffic features remain unavailable for public commercial use until provider permission, operator eligibility, security, and legal checks are confirmed.
